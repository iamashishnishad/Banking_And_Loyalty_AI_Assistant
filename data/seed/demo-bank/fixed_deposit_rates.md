# Fixed Deposit (FD) Interest Rates

Effective 1 October 2026. Rates are per annum for deposits below Rs 3 crore. Fictional sample data.

| Tenure | General public | Senior citizens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 days to 45 days | 3.50% | 4.00% |
| 46 days to 179 days | 5.25% | 5.75% |
| 180 days to 1 year | 6.25% | 6.75% |
| 1 year to 2 years | 7.10% | 7.60% |
| 2 years to 5 years | 7.00% | 7.50% |
| 5 years to 10 years | 6.75% | 7.25% |

Senior citizens (60 years and above) get an extra 0.50% on all tenures.

## Premature withdrawal

A fixed deposit can be closed before maturity. The interest paid will be the rate applicable for the period the deposit actually stayed with the bank, minus a penalty of 1%. No penalty applies to tax-saver FDs after the 5-year lock-in.

## Tax-saver FD

Tax-saver fixed deposits have a lock-in period of 5 years and qualify for deduction under Section 80C up to Rs 1.5 lakh. Premature withdrawal and loans against tax-saver FDs are not allowed.

## TDS on FD interest

TDS at 10% is deducted when FD interest exceeds Rs 40,000 in a financial year (Rs 50,000 for senior citizens). Submit Form 15G or 15H to avoid TDS if your total income is below the taxable limit.
