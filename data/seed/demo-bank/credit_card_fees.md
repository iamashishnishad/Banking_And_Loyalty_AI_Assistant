# Credit Card Fees and Charges

Fictional sample data for Demo Bank.

- Platinum Credit Card annual fee: Rs 999 + GST. Waived if annual spends exceed Rs 1.5 lakh.
- Signature Credit Card annual fee: Rs 2,999 + GST. Waived if annual spends exceed Rs 4 lakh.
- Late payment fee: Rs 500 for outstanding up to Rs 10,000; Rs 1,000 above Rs 10,000.
- Finance charges on unpaid balance: 3.5% per month (42% per annum).
- Cash withdrawal fee: 2.5% of the amount, minimum Rs 500.
- Foreign currency markup: 3.5% on international transactions.
- Card replacement fee: Rs 200 + GST.

The interest-free period is up to 50 days from the date of purchase, provided the previous statement's total amount due was paid in full by the due date.
