# Rewards FAQ (Hinglish)

## Mere reward points kab expire hote hain?

Aapke reward points credit hone ki date se 3 saal (36 mahine) tak valid rehte hain. Expiry se 30 din pehle aapko SMS aur email reminder milta hai. Expire hue points wapas nahi milte.

## Points redeem kaise karein?

Demo Bank app kholiye, "Rewards" section mein jaiye aur "Redeem" par tap kijiye. Aap points ko cashback (statement credit), flight/hotel booking, gift vouchers ya airline miles mein badal sakte hain. Cashback ke liye kam se kam 500 points chahiye.

## Ek point ki value kitni hai?

Cashback mein 1 point = 25 paise. Travel portal par flights aur hotels ke liye 1 point = 50 paise, isliye travel booking mein points ki value sabse zyada milti hai.

## Fuel aur rent par points milte hain kya?

Nahi. Fuel, wallet load, EMI conversion, cash withdrawal aur third-party apps se kiye gaye rent payment par reward points nahi milte.

## Card band karne par points ka kya hoga?

Card close karne ke baad aapke paas 60 din hain points redeem karne ke liye. 60 din ke baad bache hue points khatam ho jaate hain.

## Redemption cancel ho sakta hai?

Nahi, ek baar redemption process hone ke baad cancel nahi hota. Vouchers 3 working days mein email par aa jaate hain.
