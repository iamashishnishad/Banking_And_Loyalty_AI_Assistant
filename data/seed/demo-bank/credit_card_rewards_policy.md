# Demo Bank Credit Card Rewards Policy

All Demo Bank figures in this document are fictional sample data.

## Earning reward points

Customers earn 2 reward points for every Rs 100 spent on a Demo Bank Platinum Credit Card. Demo Bank Signature Credit Card holders earn 4 reward points for every Rs 100 spent.

Spends on dining and online groceries earn 5X reward points, capped at 2,500 bonus points per statement cycle.

The following transactions do not earn reward points: fuel purchases, rent payments made through third-party apps, wallet loads, EMI conversions, cash withdrawals, and payment of insurance premiums above Rs 50,000 in a month.

Reward points are credited within 7 working days of the transaction posting to the card account.

## Points expiry

Reward points are valid for 3 years (36 months) from the date they are credited. Points that are not redeemed within this period lapse automatically on the last day of the 36th month.

Customers receive an SMS and email reminder 30 days before any points expire. Expired points cannot be reinstated.

If the credit card is closed, unredeemed reward points must be redeemed within 60 days of closure. After 60 days they are forfeited.

## Redeeming points

Reward points can be redeemed through the Demo Bank mobile app, net banking, or by calling customer care.

- Statement credit (cashback): 1 point = Rs 0.25. Minimum redemption 500 points.
- Flights and hotels on the Demo Bank travel portal: 1 point = Rs 0.50.
- Gift vouchers from partner brands: 1 point = Rs 0.30 to Rs 0.40, depending on the brand.
- Airline miles transfer: 2 points = 1 partner airline mile, minimum 1,000 points.

A redemption fee of Rs 99 plus GST applies to each statement-credit redemption. Travel and voucher redemptions have no fee.

Redemptions cannot be cancelled once processed. Vouchers are delivered by email within 3 working days.

## Reversal of points

If a purchase is refunded or reversed, the reward points earned on it are deducted. If the account does not have enough points, the balance may become negative and future points are adjusted against it.
